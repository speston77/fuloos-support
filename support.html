Fuloos support

Questions about your records or a problem with the app? Contact Mo Al Siyabi at fuloos.support@gmail.com.

Email support

When reporting a problem

Include the Fuloos version and build, your iPhone model, iOS version, app language, and the steps that caused the issue. The app version is shown in Settings → About Fuloos. A redacted screenshot can help.

Keep bank passwords, device passcodes, full card numbers, and private financial records out of your message. Support has no remote access to your local database; you choose which details to share.

Common questions

Fuloos says it cannot open or save my data.

Keep the app installed. Check that your iPhone has free storage, then use Try again. After a failed save, Fuloos reopens committed records in a fresh session; repeat the change only after checking the saved records. If the issue continues, email support with the error screen and reproduction steps. Deleting or reinstalling the app can remove records that have no backup.

Does Fuloos connect to my bank or move money?

No. Fuloos records personal finances. Balances and forecasts reflect your opening balances and recorded entries, rather than a live bank balance. Scheduled entries do not pay bills or transfer money. Review estimates against your actual accounts.

How do bank-message Shortcuts work?

Open Settings → Automate your money → Bank SMS & Shortcuts. Enable imports and follow the in-app guide to configure a personal automation. Start with the connection test. If the selected account is unavailable, select it again in Shortcuts. Unsupported or ambiguous messages may require manual entry; verify your transaction history before retrying.

Why are exchange rates missing or out of date?

Connect to the internet and reopen Fuloos. Rates refresh when the cached table is due. Saved rates can support offline conversions; unavailable conversions are shown explicitly. Rates are indicative estimates and may differ from your bank’s settlement rates. The public table is provided by ExchangeRate-API.

How do I export or remove my records?

In Settings → Your data, choose CSV or JSON and select where to save or share the file. These files contain transaction copies; they are not a complete restorable backup of the app. Delete individual transactions from the Transactions tab. To remove all local app data in the current version, use Delete App in iPhone Settings; Offload App preserves data. Manage previously shared exports and device backups separately. In builds that include Settings → Your data → Erase financial data, you can instead confirm removal of all financial records, plans, income setup, SMS diagnostics and temporary exports. Imports and badges are disabled and setup restarts; language, currency, appearance and security choices remain. Shared exports, device backups and Shortcuts automations are managed separately.

What if device authentication fails?

Try Unlock Fuloos again and follow the iOS prompt. iOS can offer the device passcode when biometrics are unavailable or locked out. Check your device’s Face ID, Touch ID, and passcode settings. App lock protects Fuloos’s screens; separately enabled bank-message automations can still record transactions while locked.

Why does the icon badge differ from Home?

The optional icon badge shows the weekly limit rounded down to a whole currency unit. Zero removes the badge. Home shows the precise estimate. The badge updates while Fuloos runs and is hidden by privacy mode or app lock.

Is my language or currency supported?

Fuloos supports English and Arabic, with an in-app language switch. OMR, KWD, and BHD use three decimal places; JPY uses whole units. Your account currency and favourite display currency may differ, and conversions require an available rate.
