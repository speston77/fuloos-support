Privacy policy

Effective 10 November 2026 · Publisher: Mo Al Siyabi

This policy covers the Fuloos iPhone app and its support website. Contact fuloos.support@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.

Financial records stay in the app’s local storage

Fuloos stores the records you enter on your device: financial accounts and opening balances, transactions, currencies, categories, merchants, notes, budgets, recurring entries, installment plans, and savings goals. It also stores app preferences and cached exchange rates.

These records support the app’s balances, planning, forecasts, and exports. Fuloos does not upload your financial database to a Fuloos server. No Fuloos online account or bank login is created. We do not sell financial records or use them for advertising, and the app includes no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

Device backups you enable through Apple may include app data. That is separate from app-operated cloud sync, which the current version does not provide.

Bank messages and device authentication

Bank-message import is off by default. If enabled, a Shortcut you configure supplies message text for on-device parsing. Fuloos does not scan your Messages inbox. Its database stores the parsed transaction and a message-derived hash for duplicate detection, rather than the raw message body. Local import diagnostics contain a status and timestamp, not the message text.

Disable imports in the in-app Bank SMS & Shortcuts guide to stop this feature. Enabled automations may record transactions while Fuloos’s screens are locked. iOS manages Face ID, Touch ID, and passcode authentication; Fuloos receives the authentication result, not your biometric templates or passcode.

Exchange-rate requests

When a rate refresh is due, Fuloos requests a public USD exchange-rate table from ExchangeRate-API over HTTPS. It does not include your transaction amounts, bank messages, financial-account names, or notes. The provider receives your IP address and normal request metadata and handles them under its own policy. Its processing may occur outside your country. Cached rates support offline use; conversions may be unavailable when no suitable rate exists.

Exports and support messages

CSV and JSON exports are created on your device and shared only through an activity you choose. Temporary share files are cleaned up when sharing finishes. Copies you save or send are controlled by you and the receiving service.

If you email support, Mo Al Siyabi receives your email address, message, and attachments through Gmail to answer your request and investigate app issues. See Google’s privacy policy for the email service. Send only information you want to share; avoid bank credentials, full card numbers, and unredacted financial exports.

Apple diagnostics and beta testing

Apple’s system settings govern any App Store diagnostic information made available to the publisher. During TestFlight testing, Apple automatically shares beta crash and usage information with the publisher, and shares feedback you submit. We use that information to investigate problems and improve Fuloos. See TestFlight & Privacy for what Apple collects and shares. Remove private financial details from feedback screenshots.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

Local financial records remain until you delete them or remove the app’s data. You can delete transactions in the app and export transaction copies from Settings. To remove all local records and preferences in the current version, use Delete App in iPhone Settings. Offload App keeps its data. Manage shared exports and device backups separately; reinstalling cannot restore records without an available backup.

Support correspondence and any diagnostics we receive are retained only as needed for support, resolving issues, security, and applicable recordkeeping obligations. Email the address above to request access, correction, or deletion of information held by the publisher. We will explain any information that must be retained. We cannot remotely access or erase your device’s local database.

You can turn off bank-message imports and icon badges, control app lock and amount visibility, and manage system permissions in iPhone Settings.

This website

These pages add no analytics scripts, advertising pixels, cookies, or submission forms. GitHub Pages hosts the website and logs visitor IP addresses for security. See GitHub’s privacy statement. Opening an external link or sending email involves the relevant provider’s services.

Policy updates

Updates will be published here with a revised effective date. Material changes to app data practices will be reflected in the app’s disclosures. Privacy contact: fuloos.support@gmail.com · Mo Al Siyabi.
